FAQ

Common questions

Can’t find your answer? Open a question on GitHub.

How do I install the app?

The easiest way is Google Play: search for MamaMilkiva or tap the button on this page on your phone. If you don’t use Google’s store, you can download the APK from GitHub. Android may ask whether to allow installs from that source.

Is the app available in English?

Not at the moment: the app is in Slovenian. This website is available in both languages.

Does the app send my data anywhere?

No. The app doesn’t even have permission to access the internet. All entries are stored only on your phone, with no account, ads or analytics.

Can I choose when it reminds me?

Yes. You turn the feeding and pumping reminders on separately and pick an interval from 30 minutes to 12 hours for each. Both default to 3 hours.

What if I don’t allow notifications?

Logging and history work normally; you just won’t get reminders. You can turn notifications on any time in Settings.

Why might a reminder arrive late?

If Android doesn’t allow exact alarms or your phone is saving battery, a reminder may arrive a little later, but never before your interval is up.

What happens to my entries if I change phones?

Your entries stay on this phone only. Cloud backup and transfer to a new phone are turned off. Uninstalling the app deletes your entries.

Is MamaMilkiva a medical device?

No. Reminders are an organisational aid, not medical advice.
