Privacy policy

  • Effective from 14 October 2026
  • MamaMilkiva 1.0
  • App and website

This policy explains which data the MamaMilkiva app uses and where it is stored. In short: everything you log stays on your phone.

In short

  • Only on your phoneEntries are stored in a local database on your device.
  • No internetThe app has no internet permission and sends data nowhere.
  • No trackingNo ads, analytics, cookies or trackers.
  • No accountNo sign-in needed; no name or email is collected.

1Who is the controller

MamaMilkiva is an Android app developed by Denis Korinsek. For privacy questions, write to korinsekdenis+mamamilkiva@gmail.com.

2What data the app processes

The app only processes data you enter yourself:

  • the time of each feeding (date and time),
  • the start time of each pumping session (date and time),
  • your selected feeding methods (breastfeeding, pumping, formula),
  • your reminder settings: whether they are on and after how long they remind you.

The app does not collect your name, age, location or any other data about you or your baby.

3Where the data is stored

All data is stored only in a local database on your phone. The app has no server, account or sync, and no permission to access the internet.

Backups are turned off. Cloud backup and transfer to a new phone are disabled. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all entries are permanently deleted.

4Permissions

The app only asks for the permissions its reminders need. Without them, logging and history work normally.

Notifications
To show feeding and pumping reminders. On the lock screen the notification is private, with no details.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Exact alarms
So a reminder arrives at the planned time. If you don’t allow them, Android may delay it, but never send it early.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
Run at startup
So scheduled reminders are restored after a restart or a time change. Android grants this permission automatically.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

5Data sharing

We don’t send your data to anyone and we don’t sell it. The app has no ads, analytics, cookies or third-party trackers.

Because the app has no internet permission, it cannot send anything by itself. If you install it from Google Play, Google’s terms apply to the download. Android and Google Play may collect technical data under their own policies, for example about installs and crashes.

6Your rights and deletion

You can edit or delete entries at any time in History. To delete all data, clear the app’s data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Because your data never leaves your phone, we have no access to it and cannot export, correct or delete it on your behalf.

7Children

The app is meant for parents and caregivers. It collects no personal data about your baby, and the feeding times you enter stay on your phone.

8This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Fonts and images load from the same server as the page. If you pause the animations, that choice is saved only in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Links to Google Play and GitHub lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

9Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
